The Best Movies According to Saniyya

My Top Movies of All Time.

1. Spirited Away

Spirited Away is a 2001 Japanese animated fantasy film written and directed by Hayao Miyazaki and produced by Studio Ghibli. The film tells the story of Chihiro Ogino, a sullen ten-year-old girl who, while moving to a new neighborhood, enters the world of spirits and must find her way to save her parents and return to the human world. Spirited Away is widely regarded as one of the greatest animated films ever made, winning numerous awards including the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature.

2. Ex Machina

Ex Machina is a 2014 science fiction thriller film written and directed by Alex Garland. The film follows Caleb Smith, a young programmer who wins a competition to participate in a secret experiment at the headquarters of a tech company. The experiment involves interacting with an advanced artificial intelligence robot named Ava. The film explores themes of consciousness, identity, and the nature of humanity.

3. Drive

Drive is a 2011 action drama film directed by Nicolas Winding Refn and starring Ryan Gosling. The film follows an unnamed Hollywood stunt driver who moonlights as a getaway driver for criminals. When he becomes involved with his neighbor Irene and her young son, he finds himself drawn into a dangerous criminal underworld. Drive is known for its stylish direction, intense action sequences, and atmospheric soundtrack.
